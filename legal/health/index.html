English

Calio provides general nutrition, lifestyle, and wellness guidance only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

  • The nutrition estimates, insights, weekly reports, and coach messages in Calio are estimates and educational suggestions, not clinical assessments. Photo, voice, and barcode analyses are approximations and may be inaccurate.
  • Allergen flags are estimates and can be wrong. Never rely on Calio to determine whether a food is safe for you. If you have a food allergy or intolerance, always verify ingredients directly and consult a qualified professional.
  • Calio does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease, and makes no guarantee of any health or weight outcome.
  • Always seek the advice of your physician, a registered dietitian, or another qualified health provider with any questions about your health, diet, a medical condition, pregnancy, or before starting any nutrition or exercise plan.
  • If you think you may have a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services immediately.
  • Calio is not intended for the diagnosis or management of eating disorders. If you are struggling with your relationship with food, please reach out to a qualified professional or a local support line.

By using Calio you acknowledge that you understand and accept this disclaimer.
